🗓️ Action Plan Based On Research

What Top Creators Do That You Should Copy

🏆 Strategy #1: Series Over Singles

Top creators build SERIES. Not one lens — a series of 5-10 related lenses. Users who love Lens 1 will try Lenses 2-5. Your country beauty series (195 countries) IS a series. Build the most popular 20 first, announce the series, let community request next countries.

🏆 Strategy #2: Interactive Beats Passive

Every top-earning lens has an interactive element — swipe to change, tap to reveal, score system, quiz result. A beauty filter you just look at gets 30 seconds. A beauty filter with "tap to try 5 shades" gets 3 minutes. 6x longer playtime = 6x more chance of becoming a Qualified Poster.

🏆 Strategy #3: Challenge Mechanic Built In

Paige Piskin and CyreneQ never release a lens without a challenge concept. The viral hook isn't a tag — it's a question that makes people NEED to respond. "Rate my glass skin 1-10" forces a reply. "Which K-beauty look am I?" forces a share. Build the challenge INTO the lens concept, not as an afterthought.

🏆 Strategy #4: Own Underserved Markets

CyreneQ owns the Filipino/Asian cultural AR space. Nobody is owning the African cultural beauty space, the South Asian beauty space, or the Arabian luxury beauty space. These are your lanes. 400K AR creators exist but 99% target the US/European market. Emerging market beauty = yours to own.

🏆 Strategy #5: Submit to All Programs

Lens+ Payouts (new Oct 2025) + Top Performer Payouts = two income streams per lens. Submit some lenses as Lens+ Exclusive (higher earning per user but smaller audience) and some as Top Performer (mass market). Diversify across both programs. Build 2-3 new lenses per week minimum.

⚠️ What NOT to Do (Common Mistakes)

1. Building lenses that only look good in perfect lighting (fails in real use = less sharing)
2. Over-smoothing skin until it looks plastic (users stop sharing because it looks fake)
3. Lenses that only work on one skin tone (cuts your addressable audience in half)
4. No viral hook — lens releases with no challenge concept = dies immediately
5. Building for the wrong regions — focus on US, France, Saudi, UK, Germany, Canada, Australia, UAE (the 11 qualified payout regions)
